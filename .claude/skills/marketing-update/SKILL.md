# Marketing Update

Produce a skimmable update for the user's manager. Direct and factual. No filler, no praise, no hedging.

## Format (strict)

```
**Wins**
- ...
- ...
- ...

**Blockers**
- ...

**Priorities**
- ...
```

- Exactly three sections, in this order: Wins, Blockers, Priorities.
- Maximum three bullets per section. Fewer is fine. Never pad.
- One line per bullet (two at most). A manager should read the whole update in under 30 seconds.
- No intro, no sign-off, no extra headings.

## Tone

- Direct, factual, past tense for Wins, present tense for Blockers, future tense with dates for Priorities.
- Lead each bullet with the number or the outcome, then the channel or activity.
- No adjectives like "great", "strong", "significant". Let the number carry it.
- Do not invent figures. If a number is missing, ask the user or write "n/a" rather than guess.

## Bullet rules per section

**Wins**: result first, with a metric and a comparison (vs last period or vs target).
- Good: `Email campaign: 4.2% CTR vs 2.9% prior, 38 corporate leads.`

**Blockers**: what is stuck, who or what it waits on, what is needed, by when.
- Good: `Print vendor delay on brochures. Need approval on revised proof by Fri or the event kit misses the 28th.`
- Only list items the manager can help unblock or must know about.

**Priorities**: ordered by importance, each with an owner (default: user) and a date.
- Good: `Launch Q4 paid search campaign, live by 30 Oct.`

## Scope: what counts as marketing

Pull from all relevant elements; pick the three most material per section, not one per channel.

- **Traditional / offline**: print collateral, brochures, outdoor and signage, events and roadshows, trade shows, direct mail, sponsorships, PR and media, partner and agent co-marketing, sales enablement material.
- **Digital**: SEO and content, paid search and social ads, email and WhatsApp campaigns, social media, website and landing pages, marketing automation, influencer or affiliate, listings and reputation management.
- **Cross-cutting**: lead generation and conversion, CRM and pipeline contribution, brand and messaging, budget and spend vs plan, analytics and attribution, vendor and agency management.

## Process

1. Ask the user for this period's raw notes or numbers if they were not provided. Accept messy input.
2. Select the highest-impact items. Merge related items into one bullet.
3. Draft in the format above. Check: three sections, max three bullets each, every Win has a number, every Blocker has an ask, every Priority has a date.
4. Return only the update, ready to paste.
