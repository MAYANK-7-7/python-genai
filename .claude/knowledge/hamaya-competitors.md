## REGULATORY & ACCREDITATION FRAMEWORK

### IATA Accreditation
- Authorization to issue airline tickets and settle via BSP
- Requirements: Financial guarantee, office inspection, staff qualification, application fee
- FBTW status: TIDS registered ✅ — full IATA accreditation in progress
- Live payment gate: Razorpay KYC in progress (Merchant ID: TARYB9ksqJc8f7) — gated until IATA certificate

### BSP India (Billing and Settlement Plan)
- IATA's central billing system between agents and airlines in India
- Flow: Agent books via GDS → airline confirms → BSP India settles weekly
- FBTW status: Connected ✅ — billing cycle active
- BSP India billing runs on weekly cycles (Monday-Sunday settlement)
- Administrator: IATA India (New Delhi office)

### TIDS (Travel Industry Designator Service)
- IATA identifier for agencies not yet fully IATA accredited
- FBTW status: Registered ✅
- Limitation: Cannot independently settle via BSP until full IATA accreditation

### DGCA (Directorate General of Civil Aviation)
- Not directly regulated as agent, but must comply with:
  - Passenger rights rules (refund timelines, cancellation policies)
  - Charter operation rules if FBTW moves into charter
  - Fare filing or tariff changes from DGCA circulars

### GST on Air Travel (India)
- Domestic economy: 5% GST on base fare
- Domestic business/first: 12% GST on base fare
- International flights: Zero-rated (0% GST)
- Agent service fee: 18% GST on the service fee/commission
- Invoice requirement: FBTW must issue GST-compliant invoice for every booking
- GST registration: Required before FBTW can legally collect and remit GST

## FARE CONSTRUCTION BASICS

- Normal fares: Published tariff fares filed with IATA — public, bookable by any agent
- Special fares: Net fares negotiated between airline and agent/TMC — confidential, better margins
- Published fares: Bookable at listed price — FBTW earns commission or service fee
- Net fares (BSP): Agent buys at net, sells at market — margin is the spread
- Corporate fares: Negotiated between airline and corporate — agent facilitates, earns service fee
- Consolidator fares: Bulk net fares from consolidators — useful before direct airline relationships
- Fare classes: Y/B/M etc. for economy; J/C for business; F for first
- Lowest available fare (LAF): Compliance requirement for many corporate policies
- Advance purchase: Most cheap fares require booking 7-21 days in advance
