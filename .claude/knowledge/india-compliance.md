## GST 2.0 (Effective 22 September 2025)

| Category | GST Rate |
|---|---|
| Domestic Economy | 5% |
| Domestic Business / First | 18% |
| International Economy (ex-India) | 5% |
| International Business / First (ex-India) | 18% |
| SOTO (Sold Outside Ticketed Outside India) | Zero-rated |
| Agent service fee | 18% (SAC 998551) |
| UDF / ADF / PSF (statutory levies) | GST EXEMPT |

**GSTIN mandatory** for all corporate bookings — no exceptions.

## DGCA 2026 (Effective 26 March 2026)

- **48-hour look-in window** — Domestic (departure ≥ 7 days from booking)
- **48-hour look-in window** — International (departure ≥ 15 days from booking)
- **Statutory taxes ALWAYS refundable** — even on non-refundable fares
  - UDF (User Development Fee) — always refund
  - ADF (Airport Development Fee) — always refund
  - PSF (Passenger Service Fee) — always refund
  - No exceptions, no carrier override permitted
